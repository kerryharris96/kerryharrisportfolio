How is pricing structured?

Every project is scoped around your goals and the amount of work involved. Retainers are a fixed monthly fee, strategy projects are a fixed one-off fee, and campaigns are priced per campaign, with no hidden extras.

Do you offer monthly retainers or one-off projects?

Both. Monthly management is the best way to build steady growth, and one-off projects such as audits, launches and campaigns are available for businesses that aren't ready for ongoing support.

Do you create the content yourself?

Yes. I plan, film, edit and write short-form video, photography and platform-specific copy, and I run creator and UGC campaigns.

Which platforms do you work with?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and X, across organic and paid. I'll recommend the channels where your audience actually spends time, rather than spreading you thin.

Can you help beyond organic social?

Yes. I've planned and supported paid social campaigns, and I provide end-to-end support across strategy, content, websites and digital marketing.
