FAQs

Good questions.

How much do your services cost?

Every business is different, so I quote based on your goals, the channels involved and the amount of content needed. Get in touch and I'll put together a clear proposal.

Do I have to sign up for monthly management?

No. Monthly management is the best way to see steady growth, but one-off projects such as audits, campaigns or channel setups are available too.

Can you create the content as well as plan it?

Yes. I can plan, shoot, edit and write short-form content and Reels, as well as run creator and UGC campaigns.

Which platforms do you work with?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and X, across both organic and paid social.

Can you help with more than social?

Yes. I provide end-to-end support across strategy, content, websites and digital marketing, whether you are launching a new brand, refreshing your online presence or scaling up.
