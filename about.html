About me

Hi, I'm Kerry.

I'm a social media strategist with 6+ years' experience building audience growth, creator campaigns and community-led brand marketing.

I've managed social for global brands including Peugeot, Alfa Romeo, DS Automobiles and Uphold, across automotive, fintech and cryptocurrency. Since 2024 I've also worked as a freelance Marketing & Social Media Consultant, helping startups, hospitality businesses, fintech companies and independent brands grow online.

Across every project, I combine creative storytelling with data-driven decisions to create content and strategies that engage audiences, build communities and generate lasting business growth.

My mission

To help brands build meaningful digital communities through social-first storytelling, creator collaboration and platform-native content.
