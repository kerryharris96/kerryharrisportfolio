Peugeot · Launch campaign · 2023

Peugeot Presents: Inception Concept

Bringing Peugeot's vision of its electric future to London's Outernet, designed from the start to be filmed, shared and talked about.

Client
Peugeot UK
Sector
Automotive
Channels
Instagram, YouTube
My role
UK Social Media Manager

The challenge

The Inception Concept was Peugeot's statement about the future of the brand. Its UK launch had to feel as bold as the car, and reach far more people than could ever see it in person.

Key strategic decisions

  • Shift the storytelling from what the car is to how the brand makes people feel
  • Encourage creator participation to extend reach beyond paid media
  • Anchor all content to Peugeot's four brand pillars: Stunning Design, High-End Quality, Exciting Drive and Electric Leadership

Execution

  • Built social strategy into the Outernet Cube experience so it was designed for content creation
  • Supported phone photography workshops that helped visitors create high-quality UGC
  • Curated and amplified creator and visitor content across Instagram and YouTube

The results

  • 126Mtotal reach
  • 11.4Msocial media impressions
  • 76,000+in-person visitors
  • +2,000Instagram followers in one week

Campaign-wide results, delivered as part of a wider team.
