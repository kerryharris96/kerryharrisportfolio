The challenge

Small Beer Co makes mid-strength beer: a smart idea that needs explaining. The brand needed to build awareness, show off the product and the taproom, and turn local interest into a loyal, engaged audience against much bigger drinks brands.

The approach

Every Reel had a job to do. Rather than just showing the product, each one gave people something useful or entertaining, with the beer at the centre and the brewery team in front of the camera.
