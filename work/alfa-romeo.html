Alfa Romeo · UGC campaign

#WinAlfaRomeoF1Merch

Alfa Romeo's most passionate fans were on fan-run pages, not the brand's channels. So we gave them a reason to come back, and made them the stars.

Client
Alfa Romeo UK
Sector
Automotive · Motorsport
Channels
Instagram, Facebook, fan pages
My role
UK Social Media Manager

The challenge

Alfa Romeo had a highly passionate fan base, but much of that enthusiasm lived outside the official brand channels, on fan-run pages and groups. The brief was to:

  • Increase impressions and engagement without over-relying on paid media
  • Activate loyal fans in an authentic, brand-safe way
  • Bring community creativity back onto official Alfa Romeo channels

The approach

Rather than competing with the fan ecosystem, the campaign celebrated it. A UGC competition invited fans to submit Alfa Romeo F1-themed content for the chance to win official F1 merchandise, with voting mechanics built around how each platform's audience behaves.

Execution

  • Launched a UGC competition inviting fans to submit Alfa Romeo F1-themed content
  • Curated submissions and showcased them across Instagram and Facebook to celebrate the community
  • Used Instagram Story polls to streamline voting and maximise engagement
  • Built a structured Facebook voting system tailored to the platform's audience
  • Collaborated with fan-run pages to extend reach while keeping the brand consistent

The results

  • +52%impressions during the campaign
  • +90%engagement during the campaign
  • +51%customer engagement across Facebook fan communities over the following three months

The campaign's success led to expansion across more Alfa Romeo fan pages. The client called it a great success, and it became a repeatable model for future community-led campaigns.

"UGC works best when fans feel seen, valued and involved."

By respecting the existing fan ecosystem and giving the community ownership of the content, we unlocked higher engagement than traditional brand-led posting.
