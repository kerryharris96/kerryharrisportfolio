The challenge

Crypto is a fast-moving, sceptical market where trust is hard to win and easy to lose. Uphold needed to grow its audience and change how customers felt about the brand, which showed in low review scores.

The approach

Give the brand a clear, human personality, show up for the community consistently, and grow reach through partnerships with people the audience already trusted.

What I delivered

  • Developed Uphold's social media tone of voice and brand personality across all platforms
  • Led internal and external creative teams to deliver social campaigns and content
  • Managed and trained an outsourced community management and customer support team
  • Built co-marketing partnerships with brands, tokens, creators and influencers
  • Created and hosted a weekly Twitter Spaces podcast on market insights and community engagement

The results

  • 140K+social audience, up from 50K
  • 3.2★Trustpilot rating, up from 1.2
  • 4.5★iOS App Store rating, up from 2

Audience growth was a team effort that I contributed to.
